My story

Hi, I’m Noora. I’m a journalism student at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, where I study Sports Media and Communication with minors in Communication Studies and Textile, Merchandising, and Fashion Design. In January 2026 I also started my master’s in Integrated Media Communications, because one degree’s worth of curiosity was not enough.

I like stories that explain the people behind the headline. At The Daily Nebraskan I report on campus and the Lincoln community, from an exhibit that challenges victim-blaming to international student-athletes finding their footing in Nebraska. Those stories earned three third-place honors in 2026. Away from the newsroom I copy edit research and policy publications, help students in the college’s media lab, and run events for a community I care about.

This blog is where I put my love of sports and data together. Come for the soccer analysis, stay for the stories.
