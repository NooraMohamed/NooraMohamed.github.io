What’s your major doing?

Search any University of Nebraska–Lincoln major to see how many students have chosen it each fall since 2016.

    About the data: Fall-term headcounts of students by primary major, University of Nebraska–Lincoln (data request DR12573, Fall 2016–Fall 2025). Counts for a major are summed across every college and degree level that offers it; Raikes-program listings are folded into the matching major. Students’ second majors are not included, and programs that were created or retired show only the years they existed.
